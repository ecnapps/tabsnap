💡 Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

How to Split a Restaurant Bill Fairly (Without the Drama)

Learn why proportional tipping is the fairest way to divide group checks, and how TabSnap eliminates awkward dining bill disputes.

Why is proportional tipping fairer than splitting evenly?
When a table simply splits a check evenly (e.g. 50/50 or divided by the head count), someone who ordered a modest side salad and tap water ends up paying for someone else's ribeye steak, appetizers, or craft cocktails. TabSnap calculates tip and sales tax as an exact proportional percentage of each diner's actual food and beverage subtotal. That way, high spenders pay their rightful share of tip and tax, and lighter diners are never unfairly overcharged.
How are shared appetizers or pitchers divided?
If 3 people at a table of 6 share spinach dip or a round of beers, simply select those 3 diners when adding the item. TabSnap divides the item cost equally among only those diners, and applies sales tax and tip strictly to their portion of that item.
How does sales tax work with tip calculation?
In the United States, restaurant tips are standardly calculated on the pre-tax food and beverage subtotal, and state/local sales tax is added on top. TabSnap follows this standard convention, allowing you to configure your local sales tax rate (e.g. 8.5%) and distributing both sales tax and tip proportionally across each diner's order.
How do I request payments through Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App?
Enter your payment handle in the optional payment info field (e.g., "Venmo: @alex-smith"). When you finish the bill, tap "Copy Summary for Venmo & Messages". You'll get an itemized breakdown with bold text, clean emojis, diner totals, and your payment info ready to paste into your group text or payment app.
Is my dining or financial data stored on any server?
No. TabSnap runs 100% in your browser. No receipts, diner names, or payment details are ever uploaded or transmitted to any server. Your session is saved securely in your browser's LocalStorage so you never lose your progress if your phone screen locks.
